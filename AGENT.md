# AGENT.md — the WRITE model's contract for book 10 country essays

Read CONTEXT.md first (the window thesis, the method discipline, the instruments).
Book 8's voice rules apply wholesale (~/book8/AGENT.md and CONTEXT.md §Voice): quotes
verbatim or gone, banned blame register (propaganda/brainwashing/regime-as-sneer/sheep),
banned guide register (just/simply/obviously/of course), findings as human sentences,
arithmetic in receipts, causal precision — a list proves admission, never motive.

## What a country essay IS

One country's window: what its school system admits from the world's literature, through
which administrative door, and what each admission is there to do. The essay's spine is
the DOOR (mandate / menu / anthology / quota / open formula / no list) and its cargo —
never a ranking, never a score. 1,200–2,500 words; shorter where the shelf is small
(China's four can be a jewel of 1,200; Ukraine's 39 may need the full length).
Length ruling (reader, 2026-08-11): cap 2,200 words; an essay may earn up to ~3,000
only where the shelf honestly demands it, and the draft must state what earned the
overrun (a line in Limits). Anything past 3,000 goes back for compression before audit.

## The story-spine rule (reader ruling, 2026-08-11 — binding)

**The essay's protagonist is never the document, the table, or the writer's reading
process.** An essay that walks a program clause by clause ("the next section says…",
"note who is being addressed…") is a bot's reflection on a book list — the exact failure
book 8 called "an essay trapped inside an exhibit." The protagonist must be something a
reader can stand next to: a classroom scene, a physical book a pupil carries, one work
crossing the border, one author walking through two doors. The reader is inside that
story within the first screen; the documents and the table testify INSIDE it, as
evidence, when the story calls them. If you cannot find the story, the essay is not
ready to write — say so instead of writing the catalogue.
Readability discipline: inline citation freight (page refs, document numbers) moves to
Receipts wherever prose can carry the claim without it; sentences breathe; a reader who
knows nothing about curricula must never have to re-read a sentence. Hold every draft
against the shipped register of book 8's chapters (~/book8/chapters/), not against
other drafts.

## Fixed beats (light — this is an essay, not a form)

- **The door**: how a foreign book legally enters this country's classroom. Concrete:
  name the document, its issuing body, what force a row has (examable mandate vs teacher
  menu). One paragraph, human language.
- **The shelf walk**: the works themselves — grouped as the essay's argument demands
  (by errand, by source language, by what they replace), NOT by the TSV's columns.
- **The errand close-read**: at least one work read closely enough to say what feeling
  is being imported — from the document's own teaching language where available.
- **The lander**: the reader was fifteen once, in some country's classroom, with some
  list. Land there. Tagged regional landers allowed once ("If your classroom…").
- **Receipts**: document name, issuing body, year, URL, retrieval date; per-work rows
  cite the table. Any number spoken in prose must exist in the corpus table.
- **Limits**: what this table cannot say (one-year slice, document force, the
  confirm-pass caveats for THIS country — read notes/confirm-<cc>.md and honor it).

## Hard rules for this corpus

- Only repaired tables may be cited (notes/recon-<cc>.md must exist for the country,
  or the essay waits). Rows flagged unsupported are dead until re-receipted.
- Never compare raw row counts across door types as "openness".
- Author identity is written carefully: "Russian-language, Kyiv-born" beats "Russian"
  where the document's own placement is the finding (Gogol, Bulgakov).
- The three empty tables (BE, CH, NO) are essays about the door, not about absence of
  literature: the state mandates that reading happen and declines to say what.
