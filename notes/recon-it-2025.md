## Systemic memorize ruling

`yes` or `excerpt` requires an explicit defining-document mandate to learn the named text, a named excerpt, or a stated quantity of texts by heart at this level. Reading requirements, textual analysis, closed-book examinations, implied quotation learning, and general recitation culture do not meet that rule. The saved defining document mandates reading and analysis but not memorization of a named row or quantity of texts, so all existing `memorize=no` values remain unchanged.
