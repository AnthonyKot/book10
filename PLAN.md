## What this book turned out to be about

We set out asking "which outsiders does each country admit?" The corpus answered a
different, better question. There is NO world canon — no single work reaches more than
three of seventeen systems; every ministry builds its own world from scratch. And the
sharpest signal of what a state wants its children to BE is not what it makes them read —
it is what it makes them carry in the body. The memorization triangle is the heart:
Russia sets its own canon into children's memory; Kazakhstan sets the empire's; Ukraine
sets the world's; Belarus — alone in the corpus — sets both its own and its neighbour's.
The book opens there, because every reader on earth was made to memorize something at
fifteen and can check us against their own body.
