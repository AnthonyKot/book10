# School Literature Canon Scouting Report (Book 10)

This document scouts the official source documents defining the examineable school literature canon for 15–16-year-olds (typically Grade 9/10, GCSE, OГЭ, Mittlere Reife, or 2nd Year Secondary) across 11 national school systems. 

Per the design defined in [`CONTEXT.md`](../CONTEXT.md), the unit of analysis is the **enforceable LIST** — exam codifiers, set-text lists, ministry programs, and state anthologies.

## Country Scouting Reports

### 1. Russia (RU)

1. **Exact Document**: *Кодификатор элементов содержания и требований к уровню подготовки обучающихся для проведения основного государственного экзамена по ЛИТЕРАТУРЕ* (Codifier of Content Elements and Preparation Level Requirements for the Main State Exam in Literature), annexed to *Федеральная рабочая программа по учебному предмету «Литература» (5–9 классы)*.
   - **Issuing Body**: Federal Institute for Educational Measurement (ФИПИ / FIPI) & Ministry of Education of the Russian Federation (Минпросвещения РФ).
   - **Most Recent Year**: 2024 / 2025 / 2026 edition (updated under the new unified Federal Educational Program / ФОП).
   - **Working URL**: [https://fipi.ru/oge/demoversii-spetsifikatsii-kodifikatory](https://fipi.ru/oge/demoversii-spetsifikatsii-kodifikatory) (official portal; curriculum backup on [https://edsoo.ru](https://edsoo.ru)).
2. **Format**: Structured PDF list inside the official FIPI exam specification ZIP package.
3. **Approximate Size**: ~35–45 works/authors explicitly itemized for Grade 9 OGE (spanning *The Lay of Igor's Campaign*, 18th–19th century classics by Fonvizin, Griboyedov, Pushkin, Lermontov, Gogol, Turgenev, Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Chekhov, plus 20th-century poetry/prose).
4. **Concrete Finding**: In August 2023, FIPI released updated codifiers realigning literature exams with the mandatory Federal Educational Program (ФОП). 19th-century foundational classics (Pushkin, Lermontov, Gogol) were shifted entirely into the Grade 9 (OGE) codifier, removing them as direct mandatory entries from the Grade 11 (EГЭ) codifier to make room for late-20th-century literature and contemporary patriotic works (including literature on WWII and SVO). 
   - **Source URL**: [https://www.rbc.ru/society/28/08/2023/64ec8f1c9a7947514a601275](https://www.rbc.ru/society/28/08/2023/64ec8f1c9a7947514a601275).
5. **Feasibility Grade**: **EASY**. Clean, highly structured PDF table listing exact author names, work titles, genres, and examineable sections.
6. **Memorization & Tagging**:
   - **Memorization**: Mandated by the *Федеральная рабочая программа по литературе (5–9 классы)* under *Планируемые предметные результаты* (requires recitation by heart / *заучивание наизусть* of at least 3–5 poetic works per grade).
   - **Domestic vs Foreign**: Explicitly tagged in the codifier table under separate headings (*Отечественная литература* vs *Зарубежная литература*).

### 2. Ukraine (UA)

1. **Exact Document**: *Модельна навчальна програма «Українська література. 5–9 класи»* & *Модельна навчальна програма «Зарубіжна література. 5–9 класи»* (Model Educational Programs for "Ukrainian Literature" and "Foreign Literature" for Grades 5–9).
   - **Issuing Body**: Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine (Міністерство освіти і науки України — МОН).
   - **Most Recent Year**: 2022 / 2023 revised New Ukrainian School (НУШ) edition.
   - **Working URL**: [https://mon.gov.ua/osvita-1/zagalna-serednya-osvita/navchalni-programi/modelni-navchalni-programi-dlya-5-9-klasiv-nush](https://mon.gov.ua/osvita-1/zagalna-serednya-osvita/navchalni-programi/modelni-navchalni-programi-dlya-5-9-klasiv-nush) (official MON portal; summary index on [https://osvita.ua/school/program/](https://osvita.ua/school/program/)).
2. **Format**: Official PDF curriculum annexes providing grade-by-grade text lists, author selections, and learning outcomes.
3. **Approximate Size**: ~40–50 prescribed works across the two parallel Grade 9 courses (~25–30 in Ukrainian Literature: Shevchenko, Franko, Ukrainka, Kotsiubynsky, Marko Vovchok; ~15–20 in Foreign Literature: Homer, Shakespeare, Goethe, Heine, Scott, Hugo).
4. **Concrete Finding**: In August 2022, following the full-scale Russian invasion, MON Ukraine enacted a major curriculum revision that purged over 40 Russian and Soviet writers (Pushkin, Lermontov, Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Chekhov, Mayakovsky, Tsvetaeva) from the *Foreign Literature* syllabus. Works were replaced with Western and global classics, retaining only authors historically linked to Ukraine (Nikolai Gogol, Vladimir Korolenko, Mikhail Bulgakov's *Heart of a Dog*, Anatoly Kuznetsov's *Babi Yar*).
   - **Source URL**: [https://osvita.ua/school/87044/](https://osvita.ua/school/87044/) & [https://mon.gov.ua/news/onovleno-zmist-navchalnih-program-dlya-zakladiv-zagalnoyi-serednoyi-osviti](https://mon.gov.ua/news/onovleno-zmist-navchalnih-program-dlya-zakladiv-zagalnoyi-serednoyi-osviti).
5. **Feasibility Grade**: **EASY**. Clean, authoritative, machine-readable PDF curriculum standards specifying prescribed readings.
6. **Memorization & Tagging**:
   - **Memorization**: Explicitly required in the *Очікувані результати навчання* (Expected Learning Outcomes) section: Grade 9 students must recite designated poems by heart (*вивчення напам'ять*).
   - **Domestic vs Foreign**: Structurally enforced via separate mandatory subjects (*Українська література* vs *Зарубіжна література*).

### 3. Belarus (BY)

1. **Exact Document**: *Учебная программа по учебному предмету «Белорусская литература» для IX класса* & *Учебная программа по учебному предмету «Русская литература» для IX класса* (Curriculum for "Belarusian Literature" and "Russian Literature" for Grade 9).
   - **Issuing Body**: Ministry of Education of the Republic of Belarus (Министерство образования Республики Беларусь) / National Institute of Education (Национальный институт образования - НИО).
   - **Most Recent Year**: 2023 / 2024.
   - **Working URL**: [https://adu.by](https://adu.by) (National Educational Portal `adu.by`).
2. **Format**: Official PDF curriculum standards published on the national portal.
3. **Approximate Size**: ~35–45 works total for Grade 9 across two mandatory literature subjects (~20 in Belarusian Literature: Dunin-Marcinkievič, Bahuševič, Kupała, Kołas, Krapiva, Melež, Bykaŭ; ~20 in Russian Literature: Pushkin, Lermontov, Gogol, Turgenev, Tolstoy, Dostoevsky, Chekhov).
4. **Concrete Finding**: Belarus maintains a dual-canon state system requiring parallel tracks in Belarusian and Russian literature. Curriculum guidelines on `adu.by` emphasize Soviet-legacy and Patriotic War memory (e.g. WWII prose by Vasil Bykaŭ and Ivan Melež alongside Russian 19th-century classics).
   - **Source URL**: [https://adu.by](https://adu.by).
5. **Feasibility Grade**: **MEDIUM**. PDF standards exist on `adu.by`, but server access uses regional network protections.
6. **Memorization & Tagging**:
   - **Memorization**: Mandated under *Требования к уровню подготовки учащихся* (students must memorize specified poems by heart / *заучивание наизусть*).
   - **Domestic vs Foreign**: Divided into Belarusian national literature and Russian state literature; third-country foreign texts in translation are minor sub-units inside Russian Literature.

### 4. China (CN)

1. **Exact Document**: 《义务教育语文课程标准（2022年版）》 (Compulsory Education Chinese Language Curriculum Standard, 2022 Edition) & 统编版初中语文教材（七至九年级） (Unified Edition Junior High School Chinese Textbook, Grades 7–9).
   - **Issuing Body**: Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of China (中华人民共和国教育部) / People's Education Press (人民教育出版社 - PEP).
   - **Most Recent Year**: 2022 (Curriculum Standard) / 2024 unified textbook printing.
   - **Working URL**: [http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A26/s8001/202204/t20220420_619921.html](http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A26/s8001/202204/t20220420_619921.html) (MOE China) & [http://www.pep.com.cn](http://www.pep.com.cn).
2. **Format**: Statutory PDF curriculum standard (with explicit Appendix lists) + Table of Contents of the mandatory national unified textbook (*统编本 语文*).
3. **Approximate Size**: 72 core prescribed works for Grades 7–9 (specifically: **60 mandatory classical poem/prose passages for Grade 7–9 memorization in Appendix 1**, plus 12 compulsory extended classic novels under *名著导读*).
4. **Concrete Finding**: The 2017–2019 national rollout of the unified *Tongbian* (统编本) Chinese textbook eliminated all regional variants nationwide, significantly expanding "Red Classics" and revolutionary history. Edgar Snow's *Red Star Over China* (《红星照耀中国》) was made a mandatory extended reading text for all Grade 8/9 students.
   - **Source URL**: [http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/gzdt_gzdt/s5987/201708/t20170828_312480.html](http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/gzdt_gzdt/s5987/201708/t20170828_312480.html).
5. **Feasibility Grade**: **EASY**. Appendix 1 of the 2022 Standard gives a clean 60-item classical text list, while PEP textbook contents specify the 12 extended novels.
6. **Memorization & Tagging**:
   - **Memorization**: **Strictly mandated**. Appendix 1 (*附录1: 优秀诗文背诵推荐篇目*) lists 60 classical works (Confucian texts, Tang/Song poetry, classical prose) that Grade 7–9 students MUST memorize by heart (*背诵*) for the High School Entrance Exam (*Zhongkao*).
   - **Domestic vs Foreign**: Derived / implicit. Dominated by classical and modern Chinese literature (国学 / 革命传统); foreign translated works in *名著导读* (e.g. Gorky, Verne, Saint-Exupéry) are categorized as translated literature.

### 5. India (IN / NCERT)

1. **Exact Document**: *NCERT Class 10 English Literature Syllabi (First Flight & Footprints Without Feet)* & *NCERT Class 10 Hindi Syllabi (Kshitij Part-2 & Kritika Part-2 / Sparsh & Sanchayan)*, aligned with *CBSE Secondary School Curriculum 2024–2025 / 2025–2026*.
   - **Issuing Body**: National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) / Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE).
   - **Most Recent Year**: 2023–2024 / 2024–2025 Rationalized Syllabus Edition.
   - **Working URL**: [https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php) (NCERT official portal) & [https://cbseacademic.nic.in](https://cbseacademic.nic.in).
2. **Format**: Chapter indices / Table of Contents of state readers (NCERT textbooks) + CBSE Secondary Curriculum PDF annex.
3. **Approximate Size**: ~35–40 discrete works/chapters prescribed for Class 10 across English (~11 prose + 10 poems in *First Flight*, 9 stories in *Footprints*) and Hindi (~10 prose + 8 poems in *Kshitij*).
4. **Concrete Finding**: In 2022–2023, NCERT published a formal *List of Rationalised Content* reducing textbook volume post-pandemic. In Class 10 literature and social science, poems by Urdu poet Faiz Ahmad Faiz and specific chapters were removed or edited across national textbooks.
   - **Source URL**: [https://ncert.nic.in/rationalised-content.php](https://ncert.nic.in/rationalised-content.php) (and coverage on [https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/ncert-textbook-rationalisation-controversy/article66708304.ece](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/ncert-textbook-rationalisation-controversy/article66708304.ece)).
5. **Feasibility Grade**: **EASY**. The NCERT textbooks serve as the closed canon; chapter lists provide an unambiguous 1-to-1 table of examineable texts.
6. **Memorization & Tagging**:
   - **Memorization**: No explicit board-exam oral recitation mandate; CBSE Class 10 exams evaluate stanza explanation and analytical responses.
   - **Domestic vs Foreign**: Derived post-hoc. English textbooks mix Indian English authors (Arup Kumar Dutta, Vallikkannan) with international authors (Anton Chekhov, Walt Whitman, Nelson Mandela) without explicit national metadata tags in the index.

### 6. Germany (DE - e.g. Bavaria / NRW)

1. **Exact Document**: *Bavaria: LehrplanPLUS Realschule Deutsch Jahrgangsstufe 10 & ISB Lektüreempfehlungen* / *NRW: Kernlehrplan Deutsch für die Sekundarstufe I & Vorgaben Zentralprüfung 10 (ZP10)*.
   - **Issuing Body**: Staatsinstitut für Schulqualität und Bildungsforschung (ISB Bayern) / Ministerium für Schule und Bildung des Landes Nordrhein-Westfalen (MSB NRW) / Kultusministerkonferenz (KMK).
   - **Most Recent Year**: 2023 / 2024 / 2025.
   - **Working URL**: [https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachprofil/realschule/deutsch](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachprofil/realschule/deutsch) (Bavaria) & [https://www.standardsicherung.schulministerium.nrw.de/cms/zentrale-pruefungen-10/](https://www.standardsicherung.schulministerium.nrw.de/cms/zentrale-pruefungen-10/) (NRW).
2. **Format**: Competency curriculum framework (*Kernlehrplan*) + State Recommended Reading Catalogues (*Lektüreempfehlungen*).
3. **Approximate Size**: **0 centrally mandated single-text works** for Grade 10 ZP10/Mittlere Reife exams (schools choose works from recommended lists of ~20–30 titles such as Dürrenmatt's *Die Physiker*, Frisch's *Andorra*, Goethe's *Erlkönig*, Storm's *Der Schimmelreiter*).
4. **Concrete Finding**: German educational federalism (*Kulturhoheit der Länder*) yields a key structural result: at age 15–16 (Grade 10 / Mittlere Reife), neither NRW nor Bavaria mandates a single nationwide or state-wide compulsory set text in the exam specification. Examinations are strictly *kompetenzorientiert* (evaluating unseen excerpts or school-chosen texts). Mandatory centralized set-text lists (*Sternchenthemen*) only exist in upper secondary (Abitur - Grades 12/13).
   - **Source URL**: [https://www.standardsicherung.schulministerium.nrw.de/cms/zentrale-pruefungen-10/faches-vorgaben/](https://www.standardsicherung.schulministerium.nrw.de/cms/zentrale-pruefungen-10/faches-vorgaben/).
5. **Feasibility Grade**: **HARD**. No single state-wide mandatory exam codifier PDF exists at Grade 10; the corpus must substitute official state recommended reading catalogues (*Lektüreempfehlungen*) or Abitur specifications.
6. **Memorization & Tagging**:
   - **Memorization**: No state-wide exam mandate at Grade 10, though lower-grade school curricula include classical ballad recitation (Fontane, Schiller, Goethe).
   - **Domestic vs Foreign**: Categorized by genre/epoch rather than nationality; German-language originals dominate (~85%), with translated works integrated without formal tagging.

### 7. Italy (IT)

1. **Exact Document**: *Indicazioni Nazionali per i Licei (D.P.R. 15 marzo 2010, n. 89 / D.M. 211/2010)* — Specifically: *Allegato A: Primo Biennio (Classi I e II)*.
   - **Issuing Body**: Ministero dell'Istruzione e del Merito (MIM).
   - **Most Recent Year**: 2010 framework / 2024–2026 Ministerial Directives.
   - **Working URL**: [https://www.istruzione.it/indicazioni_nazionali/](https://www.istruzione.it/indicazioni_nazionali/) (official MIM portal).
2. **Format**: Statutory Curriculum Standard Annex (PDF framework defining obligatory author milestones per cycle).
3. **Approximate Size**: ~4–6 foundational canonical pillars prescribed for the *Primo Biennio* (ages 14–16): Dante Alighieri (*Divina Commedia* selections), Alessandro Manzoni (*I Promessi Sposi* — complete/selected study in Year 2), Homer (*Iliad* / *Odyssey*), and Virgil (*Aeneid*).
4. **Concrete Finding**: In spring 2026, intense public controversy arose over draft revisions to the *Indicazioni Nazionali* proposing to make Alessandro Manzoni's *I Promessi Sposi* optional at Year 2 (age 15–16). Following backlash from literary scholars, Education Minister Giuseppe Valditara formally reinstated *I Promessi Sposi* as a compulsory second-year milestone.
   - **Source URL**: [https://www.tuttoscuola.com/promessi-sposi-dante-nuove-indicazioni-nazionali-licei/](https://www.tuttoscuola.com/promessi-sposi-dante-nuove-indicazioni-nazionali-licei/).
5. **Feasibility Grade**: **MEDIUM**. *Indicazioni Nazionali* define core canonical pillars rather than a 50-item list; specific selections are expanded in school syllabi and state-approved anthologies (*Antologie del Biennio*).
6. **Memorization & Tagging**:
   - **Memorization**: Yes. Traditional Italian high school oral examination mandates memorization and recitation (*declamazione*) of key terzets from Dante's *Inferno* (e.g. Canto I, Canto V Paolo e Francesca, Canto XXVI Ulisse).
   - **Domestic vs Foreign**: Explicitly distinguished between *Letteratura italiana* (domestic national heritage) and *Epica classica / Letteratura straniera* (Greek/Latin epics and foreign literature).

### 8. United Kingdom (UK - England GCSE)

1. **Exact Document**: *AQA GCSE (9-1) English Literature Specification (8702)* (aligned with DfE *English Literature GCSE Subject Content*, DfE-00693-2014).
   - **Issuing Body**: AQA (Assessment and Qualifications Alliance) & Department for Education (DfE). (Other exam boards: Pearson Edexcel 1ET0, OCR J352, Eduqas).
   - **Most Recent Year**: 2024 / 2025 / 2026 Specification (featuring the new *Worlds and Lives* anthology).
   - **Working URL**: [https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/english/gcse/english-literature-8702](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/english/gcse/english-literature-8702).
2. **Format**: Official PDF Exam Board Specification detailing approved set-text options across Shakespeare, 19th-century novel, Modern drama/prose, and Poetry clusters.
3. **Approximate Size**: ~30 total approved set-text options across categories (schools select 1 Shakespeare play, 1 19th-century novel, 1 modern text, and 1 15-poem cluster).
4. **Concrete Finding**: To address diversity representation in the canon, AQA launched the *Worlds and Lives* poetry cluster (first examined in 2024), pairing modern diverse poets (Roger Robinson, Caleb Femi, Imtiaz Dharker, Grace Nichols) with Wordsworth and Shelley, and added diverse modern plays such as Chinonyerem Odimba's *Princess & The Hustler*.
   - **Source URL**: [https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/english/gcse/english-literature-8702/changes-for-2024](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/english/gcse/english-literature-8702/changes-for-2024).
5. **Feasibility Grade**: **EASY**. Clean, authoritative, machine-readable PDF specification listing every eligible set-text title and author.
6. **Memorization & Tagging**:
   - **Memorization**: **Closed-book exam requirement**. DfE GCSE rules require closed-book exams: students do not give oral recitations, but MUST memorize key quotes, stanzas, and structural lines from memory for essay analysis.
   - **Domestic vs Foreign**: Strict British focus. DfE rules require texts from the British Isles; translated foreign literature is excluded from GCSE set-text lists.

### 9. United States (USA)

1. **Exact Document**: *Common Core State Standards for English Language Arts & Literacy — Appendix B: Text Exemplars and Sample Performance Tasks (Grades 9–10)* & *AP English Literature and Composition Course and Exam Description (CED)*.
   - **Issuing Body**: National Governors Association (NGA) / Council of Chief State School Officers (CCSSO) / The College Board.
   - **Most Recent Year**: 2010 (Common Core Appendix B) / 2020 (AP Literature CED).
   - **Working URL**: [https://www.corestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/](https://www.corestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/) & [https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-english-literature-and-composition](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-english-literature-and-composition).
2. **Format**: PDF Appendix text exemplars + AP Course Description document.
3. **Approximate Size**: ~35 text exemplars for Grades 9–10 in Common Core Appendix B (including Homer's *Odyssey*, Shakespeare's *Romeo and Juliet*, Steinbeck's *Of Mice and Men*, Harper Lee's *To Kill a Mockingbird*, Bradbury's *Fahrenheit 451*, Wiesel's *Night*, MLK's *Letter from Birmingham Jail*).
4. **Concrete Finding**: The US has no federal ministry of education and no national exam codifier. This market-driven vacuum is filled by three proxy lists: Common Core Appendix B (advisory exemplars), College Board AP reading lists, and major district approved lists (e.g. NYC Public Schools, LAUSD). The absence of a single ministry list is itself a defining finding.
   - **Source URL**: [https://www.corestandards.org/assets/Appendix_B.pdf](https://www.corestandards.org/assets/Appendix_B.pdf).
5. **Feasibility Grade**: **HARD**. No single enforceable national list exists; the corpus must adopt Common Core Appendix B (Grades 9–10) as the primary exemplar baseline, supplemented by AP / big-district lists.
6. **Memorization & Tagging**:
   - **Memorization**: No national or state poem memorization mandate exists in US public standards.
   - **Domestic vs Foreign**: Derived post-hoc. Appendix B combines American classics with translated world literature (Homer, Ovid, Gogol, Kafka) without explicit metadata tags.

### 10. Kazakhstan (KZ)

1. **Exact Document**: *Типовая учебная программа по учебному предмету «Казахская литература» / «Русская литература» для 5–9 классов уровня основного среднего образования* (Model Curriculum for "Kazakh Literature" and "Russian Literature" for Grades 5–9).
   - **Issuing Body**: Ministry of Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan (Министерство просвещения Республики Казахстан) / Y. Altynsarin National Academy of Education (НАО им. И. Алтынсарина).
   - **Most Recent Year**: 2022 / 2023 (Order No. 399 / Order No. 548 amendments).
   - **Working URL**: [https://adilet.zan.kz](https://adilet.zan.kz) (Official Legal Information System of RK) & [https://nao.kz](https://nao.kz).
2. **Format**: Statutory Legal Document PDF / Web Annex on `adilet.zan.kz`.
3. **Approximate Size**: ~35–45 works for Grade 9 across Kazakh Literature (Abai Kunanbayev, Shakarim, Mukhtar Auezov's *Abai's Way*, Magzhan Zhumabayev, Beimbet Mailin) and Russian/World Literature (Pushkin, Lermontov, Gogol, Chekhov, Tolstoy).
4. **Concrete Finding**: Kazakhstan's updated curriculum (*Обновленное содержание образования*) integrates Kazakh national heritage with trilingual education goals, placing Abai's *Words of Edification* (Кара сөздер) and national epics at the center of Grade 9 literature.
   - **Source URL**: [https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2200029707](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2200029707).
5. **Feasibility Grade**: **EASY**. Full-text statutory documents published on `adilet.zan.kz` provide complete lists of authors and required works per grade.
6. **Memorization & Tagging**:
   - **Memorization**: Mandated under *Цели обучения* (Learning Objectives): Grade 9 students must memorize by heart (*жатқа айту / заучивание наизусть*) designated excerpts from Abai's poems, *Words of Edification*, and poems by Magzhan Zhumabayev.
   - **Domestic vs Foreign**: Explicitly separated into *Казахская литература* (domestic national canon) vs *Русская и зарубежная литература* (co-curriculum and foreign literature).

### 11. Sudan (SD)

1. **Exact Document**: *كتاب الأدب والنصوص - الصف الثاني الثانوي* (Literature and Texts Textbook - 2nd Year Secondary, age 15–16).
   - **Issuing Body**: Federal Ministry of General Education (وزارة التربية والتعليم الاتحادية) / Bakht al-Ruda National Centre for Curriculum and Educational Research (المركز القومي للمناهج والبحث التربوي - بخت الرضا).
   - **Most Recent Year**: 2020 / 2021 (National Secondary Curriculum Framework).
   - **Working URL**: [https://moe.gov.sd](https://moe.gov.sd) (Ministry portal) & educational archive [https://al-amgaad.com](https://al-amgaad.com).
2. **Format**: Printed State Textbook Table of Contents / Scanned PDF reader (*كتاب الأدب والنصوص*).
3. **Approximate Size**: ~25–30 anthologized poems and prose selections across Classical Arabic (Pre-Islamic, Umayyad, Abbasid), Modern Arab literature, and Sudanese national poetry.
4. **Concrete Finding**: In 2020–2021, Sudan's post-revolution curriculum reform led by Dr. Omer al-Qarrai (Head of Bakht al-Ruda Centre) sparked intense political debate over artwork and literary excerpts in state textbooks, leading to al-Qarrai's resignation under conservative pressure in January 2021.
   - **Source URL**: [https://www.reuters.com/article/us-sudan-politics-curriculum-idUSKBN29C2BA/](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-sudan-politics-curriculum-idUSKBN29C2BA/).
5. **Feasibility Grade**: **HARD**. No standalone digital codifier document exists on an active ministry portal; the corpus must be reconstructed from scanned/PDF state textbooks (*الأدب والنصوص*).
6. **Memorization & Tagging**:
   - **Memorization**: Mandated directly in the textbook text: poems are explicitly tagged as either **للحفظ** (Mandatory Memorization — 8–10 lines required for exam questions) or **للأداء والدراسة** (For Study/Analysis).
   - **Domestic vs Foreign**: Categorized into Classical Arabic heritage, Modern Arab literature, and Sudanese national poets (Al-Tijani Yusuf Bashir, Muhammad Said al-Abbasi). Foreign non-Arabic literature is absent from the Arabic literature subject list.

## Design Questions Analysis

### (a) Mandatory Poem Memorization

Mandatory poem memorization is a formal, written requirement in **6 of the 11 surveyed systems**:

1. **China (CN)**: Written in **Appendix 1 (附录1: 优秀诗文背诵推荐篇目)** of the *Compulsory Education Chinese Language Curriculum Standard (2022 Edition)*. Mandates verbatim memorization (*背诵*) of **60 classical poetry and prose passages** for Grade 7–9 students, directly tested on the *Zhongkao*.
2. **Russia (RU)**: Written in the *Федеральная рабочая программа по учебному предмету «Литература» (5–9 классы)* under *Планируемые предметные результаты*. Requires students to memorize by heart (*заучивание наизусть*) at least 3–5 poetic works per grade.
3. **Ukraine (UA)**: Written in the *Очікувані результати навчання* section of the *Модельна навчальна програма «Українська література» (5–9 класи)*. Requires recitation by heart (*вивчення напам'ять*) of designated poems by Shevchenko, Franko, and Ukrainka.
4. **Sudan (SD)**: Written directly above poem entries in the official state textbook *كتاب الأدب والنصوص (الصف الثاني الثانوي)*, marking specific poems as **للحفظ** (Mandatory Memorization for exams).
5. **Kazakhstan (KZ)**: Written in the *Цели обучения* section of the *Типовая учебная программа «Казахская литература» (5–9 классы)*. Requires memorization by heart (*жатқа айту*) of Abai's verses and *Words of Edification*.
6. **Belarus (BY)**: Written in the *Требования к уровню подготовки учащихся* section of the *Учебная программа «Белорусская / Русская литература» (IX класс)*.

In contrast:
- **UK (England)**: Closed-book exam rules (DfE / AQA) mandate memorizing quotations and poem structures for written essay analysis, rather than oral recitation.
- **Italy**: Oral examination tradition under *Indicazioni Nazionali* requires memorization (*declamazione*) of key Dante terzets.
- **US, India (CBSE), Germany**: Board exams assess text analysis and stanza explanation; verbatim memorization is not a centralized exam requirement.

---

### (b) Domestic vs Foreign / Translated Tagging

1. **Explicitly Tagged by State Document**:
   - **Russia (RU)**: FIPI codifier explicitly separates entries into *Отечественная литература* (Domestic) vs *Зарубежная литература* (Foreign).
   - **Ukraine (UA)**: Institutional division into two separate compulsory school subjects (*Українська література* vs *Зарубіжна література*).
   - **Kazakhstan (KZ)**: Divided into *Казахская литература* (Domestic) vs *Русская и зарубежная литература*.
   - **Belarus (BY)**: Separated into *Белорусская литература* vs *Русская литература* (with foreign translated literature as sub-units).
   - **Italy (IT)**: *Indicazioni Nazionali* explicitly distinguish *Letteratura italiana* from *Epica classica / Letteratura straniera*.

2. **Implicit / Must Be Derived Post-Hoc**:
   - **China (CN)**: Core standard presents classical/modern works as national heritage (*国学 / 革命传统*); foreign works in *名著导读* must be derived as translated literature.
   - **UK (England GCSE)**: DfE rules require texts from the British Isles; translated foreign literature is excluded from set-text lists.
   - **India (NCERT)**: NCERT textbooks mix Indian English / Hindi writers with global authors without explicit nationality metadata; must be derived.
   - **USA (US)**: Common Core Appendix B lists works alphabetically by genre without national tagging; must be derived.
   - **Germany (DE)**: Recommended reading lists group texts by epoch and genre; domestic vs foreign origin must be derived.

---

Pilot recommendation: **Russia (RU) and UK (England GCSE - AQA)** to table-ize first, because both possess perfectly explicit, machine-readable, closed-set codifier documents (FIPI PDF codifier and AQA Specification PDF) representing polar opposite canonical models — a centralized post-Soviet state ministry codifier vs a multi-board market specification with explicit diversity reforms — establishing the schema baseline for the entire eleven-country corpus.
